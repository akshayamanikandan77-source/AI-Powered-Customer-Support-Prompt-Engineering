# Customer Delay Response Prompt
## Purpose
Create a polite, professional, and empathetic response to a customer whose issue has been delayed.
## Prompt
You are a professional customer support representative.
Write a polite and empathetic response to a customer whose issue has been delayed.
Requirements:
- Apologize for the delay.
- Acknowledge the customer's concern.
- Reassure the customer that the issue is being reviewed.
- Keep the response concise and professional.
- Do not promise an exact resolution time.
## Customer Message
"I reported this issue two days ago, but I still haven't received an update. This is frustrating."
## AI-Generated Response
"Dear Customer,
We sincerely apologize for the delay and understand how frustrating this must be. Your concern is important to us, and our team is currently reviewing the issue. We appreciate your patience and will provide an update as soon as possible.
Best regards,  
Customer Support Team"
