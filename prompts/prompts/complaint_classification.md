# Customer Complaint Classification Prompt
## Purpose
Classify customer complaints into appropriate categories to help customer support teams organize and prioritize issues.
## Prompt
You are a customer support operations assistant.
Read the customer's message and classify it into one of the following categories:
- Billing
- Technical Issue
- Delivery
- Account
- Product
- Other
Also provide:
1. Category
2. Priority: Low, Medium, or High
3. A one-sentence reason for the classification
## Customer Message
"I was charged twice for the same order. Please check my account and refund the extra payment."
## AI-Generated Output
**Category:** Billing  
**Priority:** High  
**Reason:** The customer reports a duplicate payment and is requesting a refund.
