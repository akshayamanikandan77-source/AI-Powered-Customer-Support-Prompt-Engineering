# Professional Customer Email Generation Prompt
## Purpose
Generate a clear, polite, and professional email response to a customer.
## Prompt
You are a professional customer support representative.
Write a professional email responding to the customer's request.
Requirements:
- Use a polite and helpful tone.
- Clearly address the customer's concern.
- Keep the email concise.
- Avoid unnecessary technical language.
- End with a professional closing.
## Customer Message
"I received my product, but it is damaged. Please let me know how I can get a replacement."
## AI-Generated Output
**Subject: Replacement Request for Damaged Product**
Dear Customer,
We sincerely apologize that your product arrived damaged. We understand the inconvenience caused.
Please share the required order details and a photo of the damaged product so that our team can review your request and assist you with the replacement process.
Thank you for your patience and understanding.
Best regards,  
Customer Support Team
